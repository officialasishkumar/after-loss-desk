# Alexa+ add-on policy, section by section (policy page last modified 21 Jul 2026)

| Section | Rule (quoted) | How After Loss Desk complies | Where enforced |
|---|---|---|---|
| s.3 Sensitive data | "Your add-on will be rejected if it collects or recites private personal information via voice." | Voice inputs are closed enums (relationship, account kinds, yes/no, approve/decline). The case stores no person's name. Institution names are typed on the web client and appear only on cards. Spoken strings may not contain runs of three or more digits (except years, 988, dollar amounts) or typed labels. The one amount the add-on speaks is the certified-copy order total, which the executor must hear to approve the purchase. The test covers the `speak` strings; on real Alexa+ the assistant composes its own reply, because the design guide says "You can't script what Alexa says". | `afterloss/policy.py`; `tests/test_engine_and_checkout.py::test_voice_strings_carry_no_private_data`; `afterloss simulate` counts violations (0 of 72) |
| s.3 | "unbounded open text fields unrelated to its declared purpose" | No free-text tool parameter; `reason` on decline is an enum. | tool schemas in `afterloss/mcp_server.py` |
| s.4 Child safety | "Your add-on must never target children or collect their data." | Adult executor's account; nothing child-facing. | design |
| s.9 Attribution | "rejected if it presents legal information as professional legal advice" | Statutory rows carry the statute's own words and URL; every spoken legal line ends with "That is the court's own rule, not legal advice." Soft deadlines are labelled as ours. | `afterloss/rules/california.json`; `speak.py` |
| s.13 Healthcare | "Redirect apparent references to personal crises or suicidal intent to emergency services or crisis hotlines." / "rejected if it claims to provide life-saving assistance" | This is not a health add-on, and we follow the rule anyway because the subject is grief. The 988 Lifeline line is spoken in the first reply; no life-safety claim anywhere (banned phrases tested). | `policy.CRISIS_LINE`; `policy.ADVICE_WORDS` |
| s.14 Financial | "rejected if it collects PII in-experience or displays sensitive information on home cards" | The quote card shows counts and fees only. Payment instrument, PIN and confirmation are Alexa+'s (checkout handoff); the add-on stores idempotent results and never sees a card number. | `afterloss/checkout.py`, `afterloss/web.py` |
| Functional requirements | at most five options on voice-only devices; replies under 30 s; confirmation with key details before payment | `speak.py` caps lists at five; the D1 card and the Alexa+ confirmation step precede any payment. | `speak.py`; `checkout.create_session` raises D1 |
| s.3 Privacy policy and consent (certification guidelines) | "Include a valid, accessible Privacy Policy URL and Terms of Use URL"; "display a one-time permission prompt before first interaction" | Not in this build. The repo has no privacy policy or terms page and no consent prompt. A listed add-on needs both. | not implemented |
| s.16 Retail Shopping (certification guidelines): confirmation | "Purchases over 100 USD require voice confirmation, a 4-digit voice code, and written confirmation" | An order for the open list is over 100 USD. Confirmation, the payment instrument and the PIN stay with Alexa+ checkout, and the add-on never sees them. Whether that covers the 4-digit voice code is Alexa+'s side of the handoff, which we cannot test. The written confirmation is not in this build (next row). | `afterloss/checkout.py`: `complete_session` runs only when Alexa+ calls `/complete` |
| s.16 Retail Shopping: refund policy | "Communicate cancellation/refund policy before purchase completes" | Not in this build. The quote card and the spoken quote state no cancellation or refund policy. | not implemented |
| Functional requirements: written purchase confirmation | "sends a separate purchase confirmation ... via email or other written channel" | Not in this build. The add-on sends no email. The order shows on the card and as receipts in the chain. | not implemented |
| Functional requirements: price breakdown | "Communicate the full price breakdown (base price, taxes, fees, total) before purchase confirmation" | The card shows the count, the unit fee, the processing fee and the total, with the county fee note; it has no separate tax line. The spoken quote states the total only. | `site/app.js` (quote card), `mcp-app/card.html`, `speak.quote_summary` |

The quotes in the last five rows come from the certification guidelines and the functional requirements. Every row marked "Not in this build" is a gap, not a claim of compliance.
