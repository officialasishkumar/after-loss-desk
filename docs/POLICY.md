# Alexa+ add-on policy, section by section (policy page last modified 21 Jul 2026)

| Section | Rule (quoted) | How After Loss Desk complies | Where enforced |
|---|---|---|---|
| s.3 Sensitive data | "Your add-on will be rejected if it collects or recites private personal information via voice." | Voice inputs are closed enums (relationship, account kinds, yes/no, approve/decline). The case stores no person's name. Institution names are typed on the web client and appear only on cards. Spoken strings may not contain runs of three or more digits (except years, 988, dollar amounts) or typed labels. | `afterloss/policy.py`; `tests/test_engine_and_checkout.py::test_voice_strings_carry_no_private_data`; `afterloss simulate` counts violations (0 of 72) |
| s.3 | "unbounded open text fields unrelated to its declared purpose" | No free-text tool parameter; `reason` on decline is an enum. | tool schemas in `afterloss/mcp_server.py` |
| s.4 Child safety | "Your add-on must never target children or collect their data." | Adult executor's account; nothing child-facing. | design |
| s.9 Attribution | "rejected if it presents legal information as professional legal advice" | Statutory rows carry the statute's own words and URL; every spoken legal line ends with "That is the court's own rule, not legal advice." Soft deadlines are labelled as ours. | `afterloss/rules/california.json`; `speak.py` |
| s.13 Healthcare | "Redirect apparent references to personal crises or suicidal intent to emergency services or crisis hotlines." / "rejected if it claims to provide life-saving assistance" | The 988 Lifeline line is spoken in the first reply; no life-safety claim anywhere (banned phrases tested). | `policy.CRISIS_LINE`; `policy.ADVICE_WORDS` |
| s.14 Financial | "rejected if it collects PII in-experience or displays sensitive information on home cards" | The quote card shows counts and fees only. Payment instrument, PIN and confirmation are Alexa+'s (checkout handoff); the add-on stores idempotent results and never sees a card number. | `afterloss/checkout.py`, `afterloss/web.py` |
| Functional requirements | at most five options on voice-only devices; replies under 30 s; confirmation with key details before payment | `speak.py` caps lists at five; the D1 card and the Alexa+ confirmation step precede any payment. | `speak.py`; `checkout.create_session` raises D1 |
