      Five gates, and nothing else

      Before every action the governor names the gate it would cross. Crossing one raises a card and stops. Everything else is clerical and runs on one standing authorisation, receipted.

      How it works

      1. Six answers by voice. Relationship, state, will or not, which kinds of accounts. Closed lists, no names, no numbers.
      2. The ledger, by code. A rule table with a source per row turns those answers into who to tell and by when. Deadlines are date arithmetic from the date of death.
      3. Routine work on one authorisation. Notices with copies, phone scripts, chasing silence, certified-mail escalation. Drafts are templates with facts filled by code.
      4. Five gates, before every action. D1 money D2 original D3 signature D4 irreversible D5 objection. One card per task and gate. Nothing gated runs without an approved decision; the receipt chain is scanned for it.
      5. Certified copies through Alexa+ checkout. The add-on quotes; Alexa+ confirms, picks the instrument and takes the PIN; Idempotency-Key results are kept 24 h.
