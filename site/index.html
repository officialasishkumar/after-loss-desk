      Five steps

      1. Six answers by voice. Closed lists, no names, no numbers.
      2. The ledger, by code. A sourced rule table turns the answers into who to tell and by when.
      3. Routine work on one authorisation. Notices, scripts, chasing, escalation. Templates filled by code.
      4. Five gates, before every action. One card per task and gate; the receipt chain is scanned for the approval.
      5. Certified copies through Alexa+ checkout. The add-on quotes; Alexa+ confirms, pays and takes the PIN.
